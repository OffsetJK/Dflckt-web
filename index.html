DFLCKT

Project status

A good idea
found good company.

DFLCKT began as an experiment in giving drivers meaningful choices between travel time and exposure to roadside surveillance.

After building a working proof of concept, we found that several other developers were already bringing capable versions of the same idea to market—often free or at very low cost.

Rather than build another version of something that already exists, development of DFLCKT has been paused. The project, domain, and work are being preserved in case the landscape changes.

Looking for this capability today?

Two projects worth considering.

FlockHopper

The strongest alternative we've personally tested. It offers practical surveillance-aware routing and turn-by-turn navigation.

View FlockHopper

Peripheri

Another promising project with a broader surveillance-awareness approach. Availability, features, pricing, and privacy practices may change as the product develops.

A note before using either

DFLCKT has no affiliation with either developer and receives no compensation for mentioning them. These are not guarantees or formal endorsements. Review current features, coverage, terms, and privacy practices and decide what's appropriate for you.

Thanks to everyone who shared data, ideas, and encouragement. It was a worthwhile experiment.
