Privacy should include us

Your privacy shouldn't cost you your privacy.

DFLCKT is being designed to not build a history of where you drive.

A navigation product about surveillance should not quietly become another surveillance product. Route and location information should be used only as necessary to provide the immediate service—not to create a behavioral profile of where you go.

As DFLCKT develops, what we collect and why will be explained in plain language. Commute and route history will not be sold. We can't sell route history we don't retain.

A broader view

Not every roadside camera does the same thing.

ALPR / plate readersSystems designed to identify and index vehicle plates.
Traffic-management camerasUsed for road and incident management; recording and retention vary by system.
Automated enforcementRed-light, speed, and other enforcement systems where applicable.
Other / unknown capabilityDocumented roadside observation where capabilities are incomplete or uncertain.

DFLCKT distinguishes documented facts from inference. We will not claim a camera does not record unless authoritative system-specific information supports that statement.
