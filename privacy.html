Privacy should include us

Your route is yours.

A navigation product about surveillance should not quietly become another surveillance product.

DFLCKT is being designed to provide route comparisons without building a behavioral history of where you drive.

What that means

No route-history business model. Precise commute and route history will not be sold.

Use what is necessary. Location and route information should be processed only as necessary to provide the immediate service.

Plain language. As the product develops, what DFLCKT collects, why it is needed, and how long it is retained will be explained clearly.

No false promises. DFLCKT will distinguish documented facts from inference, both in its surveillance data and in its privacy claims.

Before launch

This page describes DFLCKT's product principles while the service is in development. A complete privacy policy describing the production application's actual data flows, service providers, retention periods, user controls, and applicable legal rights will be published before public account or route-processing features launch.
